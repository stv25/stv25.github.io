Privacy Policy — Canais

Last updated: October 4, 2026

"Canais" is a private tool used only by its owner to manage his own YouTube channels (editing video descriptions, posting comments and reading channel analytics) through the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API.

Data accessed. Only data from YouTube channels owned by the app owner: video metadata, descriptions, comments and channel analytics.

Use. The data is used solely to manage those channels. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage. OAuth tokens and description backups are stored locally on the owner's computer. Nothing is stored on external servers.

Revoking access. Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).
